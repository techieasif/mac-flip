macdot

Dot-matrix widgets that flip like a departures board.

World clocks, a big clock, the date, year progress, a countdown, and a message you write. This page is the browser version of the Mac desktop widgets.

Hours

The preview steps forward a minute every few seconds so you can see the flaps. On the desktop, widgets wake about once a minute and sleep the rest of the time.

On the desk

Every widget, in a different theme. Drag the same set onto a Mac desktop from Edit Widgets.

Eight themes

Midnight, Obsidian, Amber, and Phosphor glow. Paper, Matcha, Lemon, and Sky stay flat.

Personal build for macOS 14 and later. No account, no analytics, settings stay on the Mac.
